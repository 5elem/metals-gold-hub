# Dollar holds near 2-month high as markets weigh rate hikes, Iran diplomacy

## Summary
The dollar index (**DXY**) held near about **100.79 (+0.24%)**, its strongest area in roughly two months. EUR traded near **$1.142**, USD/JPY near **157.8**. The same Reuters wrap notes Brent again above about **$99** amid US–Iran/UNGA headlines, so FX and oil are moving together with the rates story.

## Why it matters
A strong USD is a direct mechanical headwind for gold priced in dollars; this card pairs with the spot Au print to show *why* the metal is soft despite oil/geopolitical noise.
