# Gold lacklustre as higher-for-longer rate outlook weighs on sentiment

## Summary
Spot gold was quoted around **$4,345.55/oz (−0.2%)** in the Asian session snapshot (Reuters, 23 Sep ~04:35 GMT), with COMEX December near **$4,383.10**. Reuters links the soft tone to expectations that major central banks will keep rates higher for longer amid sticky inflation, while a firmer dollar makes bullion more expensive for non-USD buyers.

## Why it matters
This is the core price print and narrative for the day: Au is trading under a rates-and-dollar headwind rather than a pure geopolitical bid. Site agents should treat this as the primary market anchor card.
