# Fed's Collins says she supported rate hike, warns of elevated inflation

## Summary
Boston Fed President **Susan Collins** (22 Sep) said she supported the latest rate increase and sees a higher chance of scenarios where inflation runs **notably above 2%**. The Fed’s most recent move took the funds target to **3.75%–4.00%**; projections have pointed to the possibility of another hike later in the year. Collins framed policy as needing to stay **somewhat more restrictive** to bring inflation back to target.

## Why it matters
Official confirmation of a hawkish, higher-for-longer bias reinforces pressure on non-yielding gold and helps explain why Au weakens even when geopolitical risk is elevated.
